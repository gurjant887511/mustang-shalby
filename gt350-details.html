Track-Ready Performance with Racing Heritage

The Ford Mustang Shelby GT350 is a track-focused variant designed for serious performance enthusiasts. With its flat-plane crankshaft V8 engine, independent rear suspension, and race-inspired handling, the GT350 delivers an authentic connection between driver and machine.

🔥 Engine & Performance

5.2L Flat-Plane Crankshaft V8

  • 526 horsepower
  • 429 lb-ft torque
  • Naturally aspirated (no supercharger)
  • High-revving engine (8,200 RPM redline)
  • Iconic V8 wail at high RPM
  • 6-speed manual transmission

Performance Numbers

  • 0–100 km/h: ~3.9 seconds
  • Top Speed: around 275 km/h (170 mph)
  • Track Focus: Built for Nürburgring lap times

🏁 Track Heritage

  • Independent rear suspension (IRS) for better handling
  • Tested on the Nürburgring
  • Lightweight construction
  • Race-tuned brakes
  • Track-focused suspension geometry
  • Direct connection to racing heritage

⚙️ Transmission

6-Speed Manual

  • Pure driver engagement

  • Short-throw shifter feel
  • No automatic option (manual only)
  • Perfect for enthusiasts

Braking System
