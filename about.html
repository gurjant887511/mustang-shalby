About Shelby & Mustang Shelby

A Legacy of Performance, Innovation, and Racing Excellence Since 1962

Shelby American, Inc. - The Beginning

Shelby American, Inc. is a high-performance automobile manufacturer founded in 1962 by legendary racing driver and automotive designer Carroll Shelby in Los Angeles, California. The company began with a simple mission: to build powerful American sports cars that could beat Europe's best machines on the racetrack.

What started as a dream in a small garage has evolved into one of the most iconic names in automotive history, representing excellence, performance, and American engineering prowess.

How Shelby Started - The Carroll Shelby Story

Carroll Shelby was a successful race car driver who won the 1959 24 Hours of Le Mans, one of the most prestigious endurance racing events in the world. Due to health issues he retired from racing, but his passion for performance cars continued burning inside him.

In 1962, he teamed up with AC Cars (UK) and Ford to create the iconic AC Cobra, a lightweight body combined with Ford's big V8 engine. This car made Shelby world-famous and proved that American muscle could dominate international racing, beating European sports cars on their own turf.

The success of the AC Cobra established Shelby American as a force to be reckoned with in the automotive world and set the stage for even greater achievements.

The Birth of Mustang Shelby

In 1965, Ford approached Carroll Shelby with an exciting proposition: transform the Ford Mustang into a true high-performance racing machine. This collaboration led to the birth of the first Shelby Mustang GT350, which featured:

  • Lightweight body modifications for better performance
  • Track-focused suspension and braking system
  • Upgraded V8 engine with increased horsepower
  • Signature racing stripes that became iconic
  • Functional air scoops and aerodynamic enhancements

Just two years later in 1967, Shelby introduced the more powerful GT500, equipped with a big-block 428 V8 engine that delivered raw American muscle. These models became legends and shaped the future of American muscle cars forever.

Enhanced Mustang with extreme performance upgrades and aggressive styling

Shelby GT

Performance-enhanced models with signature Shelby engineering

Continuation of the legendary AC Cobra lineage with modern engineering

The brand stands for power, racing heritage, and premium American craftsmanship. Every vehicle bearing the Shelby name carries the spirit of Carroll Shelby's revolutionary vision.

Why Mustang Shelby Is Special

Mustang Shelby represents more than just a car—it's a symbol of American performance and racing excellence. Here's what makes it truly special:

  • Hand-built performance upgrades crafted by experts with decades of experience
  • Racing DNA inherited directly from Carroll Shelby's Le Mans victories
  • Iconic styling with stripes, vents, and aerodynamic designs that turn heads
  • Powerful engines—some of the most advanced ever in a muscle car, reaching 760 HP
  • Limited production numbers, making them collectible, exclusive, and valuable investments
  • Legacy of innovation and performance that spans over 60 years
  • Uncompromising commitment to speed, style, and driving experience
