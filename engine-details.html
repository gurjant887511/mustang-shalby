Supercharged 5.2L V8

760 Horsepower of Pure Power & Performance

Back to Features

The Supercharged 5.2L V8 is one of Ford's most powerful production engines, famously used in the 2020–2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500. It is built for extreme performance, combining high horsepower, advanced engineering, and race-car-level components.

🔥 Engine Overview

Fuel System
Direct Injection
  • Engine Type: 5.2-liter V8
  • Induction: Eaton 2.65-liter roots-type supercharger
  • Horsepower: 760 hp
  • Torque: 625 lb-ft (847 Nm)
  • Fuel System: Direct fuel injection
  • Compression Ratio: Around 9.5:1
  • Redline: About 7,500 RPM

This engine is internally known as the "Predator" V8.

⚙️ Key Features

1. Supercharger Power

  • Equipped with a 2.65L Eaton TVS R2650 supercharger.
  • Provides instant boost and massive acceleration.
  • Creates a unique whining sound at high RPM.

2. High-Strength Construction

  • Aluminum engine block to reduce weight.
  • Forged steel crankshaft for durability.
  • Strengthened pistons and connecting rods for high boost pressure.

3. Cooling System

The engine includes:

  • Dual air-to-liquid intercoolers
  • High-flow cooling channels
  • Large front air intakes

These systems prevent overheating during track use.

4. Advanced Exhaust System

  • Active valve exhaust
  • Produces an aggressive, deep muscle-car sound
  • Tuned specifically for high performance

The power delivery is extremely quick because of the supercharger.

🏎️ Transmission

  • Paired with a 7-speed dual-clutch transmission (DCT)
  • Shifts in milliseconds
  • Provides smoother power delivery compared to manual

🔊 Sound Characteristics

  • Deep, aggressive V8 rumble at idle
  • Loud, powerful roar at acceleration
  • Distinctive supercharger whine

🛠️ Applications

Mainly used in:

  • Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 (2020–2022)

This engine is currently one of the most powerful V8s Ford has ever produced for a road car.

📌 Why It's Special

  • Very high power output for a factory V8
  • Strong reliability with high-quality internal components
  • Impressive balance of speed, torque, and handling
  • Track-ready performance directly from the showroom
