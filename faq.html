Popular Questions

Everything you need to know about our legendary performance vehicles

What is the horsepower of the Mustang Shelby GT500?

The Mustang Shelby GT500 features 760 horsepower from its 5.2L supercharged V8 engine, making it the most powerful street-legal Ford ever produced. This incredible power comes from the Eaton 2.65L supercharger and advanced fuel injection system.

What is the 0-60 time of the GT500?

The Mustang Shelby GT500 can accelerate from 0-60 mph in approximately 3.3 seconds, with a top speed over 180 mph. This makes it one of the fastest production vehicles available.

What is the difference between GT500 and GT350?

The GT500 produces 760 HP with a supercharged V8, optimized for straight-line performance and street use with the 7-speed DCT. The GT350 produces 526 HP with a flat-plane crank V8, specifically designed for track performance and cornering with a racing-focused suspension.

What transmission does the GT500 have?

The GT500 is equipped with a 7-speed TREMEC dual-clutch transmission (DCT) that delivers lightning-fast gear shifts similar to supercars. This transmission provides smooth power delivery and is not available in manual form.

What is the top speed of the GT500?

The Mustang Shelby GT500 has a top speed of over 180 mph (290 km/h), with professional drivers achieving speeds up to 190+ mph under controlled track conditions.

How much does a Mustang Shelby GT500 cost?

The base price for a Mustang Shelby GT500 typically starts around $70,000-$75,000 USD. Models with the Carbon Fiber Track Pack and additional customizations can exceed $100,000+.

What are the available Shelby models?

The main Shelby models include the GT500 (760 HP street performance), GT350 (526 HP track-focused), and the Super Snake (enhanced customizations with various engine options). Each model is designed for different performance preferences.

Is the GT500 track-ready out of the box?

While the GT500 can handle track driving with its performance suspension and braking system, the GT350 is specifically designed for track performance. The GT500 is more optimized for street and drag strip performance.

What is Shelby American?

Shelby American is a high-performance automobile manufacturer founded by legendary racing driver Carroll Shelby in 1962. The company specializes in building and customizing high-performance vehicles, particularly Mustangs, and continues to operate from Las Vegas, Nevada.

What engine is in the Mustang Shelby?

The GT500 features a 5.2L supercharged V8 engine (Predator). The GT350 also has a 5.2L V8 but with a flat-plane crank design instead of supercharging, producing 526 HP instead of 760 HP.

Can I customize my Mustang Shelby?

Yes, Shelby offers various customization options including paint colors, interior upgrades, performance packages, and aerodynamic enhancements. Many options are available through the Super Snake and other special editions.
