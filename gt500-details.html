Ford Mustang Shelby GT500

The Most Powerful Mustang Ever Built

The Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 is the most powerful, fastest, and most track-focused Mustang ever produced by Ford. It represents the top level of Mustang engineering—combining American muscle with modern supercar technology.

  • Model: Ford Mustang Shelby GT500
  • Production Years: 2020–2022 (latest generation)
  • Engine: 5.2L Supercharged V8 (Predator)
  • Horsepower: 760 hp
  • Torque: 625 lb-ft (847 Nm)
  • Drivetrain: Rear-wheel drive (RWD)
  • Transmission: 7-speed Dual-Clutch Transmission (DCT)
  • Type: High-performance muscle/sports car

🔥 Engine & Performance

5.2L SUPERCHARGED "PREDATOR" V8

  • 760 horsepower
  • 625 lb-ft torque
  • Eaton 2.65L supercharger
  • Direct injection
  • High-strength internal components

Performance Numbers

  • 0–100 km/h: ~3.3 seconds
  • Top Speed: around 290 km/h (180 mph)
  • Quarter Mile: ~10.7 seconds
  • Braking (100–0): Extremely strong Brembo brakes

The GT500 is one of the most powerful factory Mustangs ever made.

🏎️ Transmission

7-Speed Tremec Dual-Clutch Transmission (DCT)

  • Lightning-fast gear shifts
  • Similar technology used in supercars
  • No manual option
  • Provides instant acceleration and smooth power delivery

⚙️ Suspension & Handling

Suspension

  • MagneRide adaptive dampers
  • Race-tuned chassis
  • Wider track for stability

Handling Abilities

  • High-speed cornering
  • Strong grip
  • Very balanced for a high-horsepower RWD car

🛑 Brakes

Brembo Braking System

  • Huge 16.5-inch front rotors
  • 6-piston calipers
  • Excellent stopping power even at high speeds

🎨 Design & Exterior

Exterior Highlights

  • Aggressive front fascia
  • Large rear wing (Carbon Fiber Track Pack)
  • Massive front grille for cooling
  • Dual hood vents with air extraction
  • Wide body stance
  • Quad exhaust tips

Designed not just to look powerful—but to improve aerodynamics and cooling.

🪑 Interior Features

  • Recaro sport seats (optional)
  • Flat-bottom steering wheel
  • Digital instrument cluster
  • Touchscreen with SYNC infotainment
  • Leather and suede materials
  • Carbon fiber trim (Track Pack)

Interior is a mix of sporty and premium.

🎛️ Driving Modes

  • Normal
  • Sport
  • Track
  • Drag Strip
  • Wet/Slippery

Each mode changes throttle, steering, suspension, and transmission behavior.

🧪 Key Technology

  • Launch control
  • Line lock (burnouts)
  • Traction management system
  • Active valve performance exhaust
  • Real-time vehicle performance monitoring

🏁 Special Edition / Packages

Carbon Fiber Track Pack

  • Carbon fiber wheels
  • GT4-style carbon rear wing
  • Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 tires
  • Rear seat delete
  • Extra performance upgrades

This is the most extreme version of the GT500.

💰 Price Range

  • Base GT500: around $70,000–$75,000 USD
  • With Track Pack: can exceed $100,000+ USD

📌 Why the Shelby GT500 Is Special

  • Most powerful production Mustang ever
  • Supercar-level acceleration
  • Legendary Shelby heritage
  • Brutal power + modern technology
  • One of the best-sounding V8s
  • It's a true American performance icon
